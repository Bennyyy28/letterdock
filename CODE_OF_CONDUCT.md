# Community guidelines

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable.

Keep issues and pull requests relevant to Letterdock. Do not share private mail or credentials, impersonate others, or use the project to facilitate spam or abuse.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. Report sensitive conduct concerns through GitHub's private Report abuse/support tools rather than posting personal details publicly. These guidelines apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces maintained by the project.
